Elliott Rosenberg is a designer
based in Brooklyn, NY.

My skillset lives at the intersection of creative exploration and technical precision: building the systems, workflows, teams, and infrastructure that make design projects thrive. Trained in furniture design at RISD and computer science at Brown, I manage teams working across physical product, digital software, and editorial design. Currently running Waiting Magazine, celebrating New York's working class creatives. At Siegel, I managed cross-functional teams and served as a translator between executive leadership, design staff, and institutional clients, building scalable accountability and operational systems.

I'm motivated by the challenge of helping to sustainably build organizations that make people's lives easier, better, and more fulfilling.

Outside of client work I like to play ultimate frisbee, watch improv, and sketch in my journal.
