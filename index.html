Elliott Rosenberg

Designer and operator

My work lives at the intersection of creative exploration and technical precision: I build the systems, workflows, teams, and infrastructure that help design projects ship. Trained in furniture design at RISD and computer science at Brown, I work across physical product, digital software, and editorial design, comfortable moving between a CAD file, a codebase, and a press check in the same week.

In the short start to my career, I've built and launched a studio-management platform on my own, started a magazine that reached 50-plus Barnes & Noble locations, manufactured a sold-out run of lamps through vendors in China, and built the brand, marketing, and sales systems behind a fast-growing architecture firm. I recently served as Director of Product at Oneg Home, and I run Perennial, my own design practice.

I'm motivated by the challenge of sustainably building organizations that make people's lives easier, better, and more fulfilling.

Who I work with

Founders and small senior teams who need someone to own the cross-functional problems that don't have a clear owner yet. People who value range and a bias toward shipping over a clean title, and who work with both technical and creative people without needing a translator between them.

Where I thrive

Early-stage and 0→1 work: resource-constrained, fast-moving, ambiguous. I do my best work at the seam between physical and digital, where a problem touches hardware, software, and brand at once. I'm a poor fit for large organizations with narrow lanes and settled process, or for roles built around going deep on a single tool.

What I deliver

Shipped products and systems, not decks about them. The operational backbone: the workflows, tracking, accountability, and vendor and manufacturing management that let a small team move quickly. The artifacts that create clarity: documents, tools, sites, and decks. And when it's needed, I can build the software, manufacture the hardware, and produce the brand myself.
